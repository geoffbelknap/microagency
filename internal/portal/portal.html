Available providers

Your operator published these and fixed each one's target. Connecting sends you to the provider to sign in; the grant comes back to the gateway.

What this gateway does with your access
  • The credential stays in the gateway. Your provider grant is stored by microagency and used on your behalf. It is not shown on this page, and it is not given to the agent or the model.
  • The connection is private to you. It is bound to the account you signed in with. Other users of this gateway cannot list it, call it, or reach its data.
  • The operator chose what is connectable. You can only connect a provider that has been published here, at the target the operator fixed, with the parameters and scopes the operator allowed.
  • Every call is recorded. Tool calls made against your connections are written to the gateway's audit log.
